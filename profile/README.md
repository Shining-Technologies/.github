## Our products

We build and run our own software, not only client work.

### [The Hivez](https://thehivez.com)

A virtual office for people and AI agents, formerly Remote Desk. Sit at a desk on a shared floor plan, walk into a room to talk, and keep chat, meetings, tasks, docs and whiteboards in the same place.

- Seats and rooms with real presence, voice and video that follow where you sit
- AI meeting notes written live, an AI workspace, and AI employees that work alongside the team
- Projects, tasks, docs, Drive, whiteboards, forms and time tracking
- Web, desktop and mobile apps

[Open the app](https://app.thehivez.com) · [Status](https://status.thehivez.com)

### [Vistra](https://vistra.report)

Inspection and field reporting for teams that document real-world work. Photograph the site, let AI draft the descriptions, and send a branded PDF, Word file or share link before you leave.

[Open the app](https://app.vistra.report) · [Status](https://status.vistra.report)
